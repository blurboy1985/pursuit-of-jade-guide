👥 Characters

Xie Zheng 谢征

Marquis of Wu'an · Male Lead
Zhang Linghe 张凌赫

On the surface a frail scholar "Yan Zheng." In reality the fallen Marquis of Wu'an, seeking revenge for his family's massacre 17 years ago. Becomes Changyu's matrilocal husband in a marriage of convenience that turns into genuine love.

Fan Changyu 范长玉

The Butcher's Daughter · Female Lead
Tian Xiwei 田曦薇

Feisty, resilient, with superhuman strength. Orphaned and raising her younger sister alone. Enters a fake marriage to protect her home, later becomes a skilled warrior and "Flower-Crowned General." Her father's true identity is the key to the 17-year mystery.

Yu Qianqian 俞千千

Restaurant Owner · Changyu's Best Friend
Snow Kong 孔雪儿

Owner of Yixiang Restaurant. A resourceful woman with a hidden son (Bao'er) — who turns out to be the true Imperial heir. In the novel, she's a modern-day transmigrator. Ends up as Empress Dowager.

Qi Min / Sui Yuanhuai 齐珉

Rice Merchant / Rebel Prince
Deng Kai 邓凯

Obsessed with Qianqian. Actually the true Imperial Grandson, swapped at birth during a palace fire. Raised by Prince Changxin as his son. His ambition to seize the throne leads to his downfall.

Li Huai'an / Wen Kan 李怀安

Martial Commandant of Jizhou
Ren Hao 任豪

Nobleman tied to powerful families, trained alongside Xie Zheng under General He. Quietly harbors feelings for Changyu. Becomes General after Gen. He's death.

Qi Min (White Hair) 👤

The True Imperial Grandson
Deng Kai 邓凯

His striking white hair and cold gaze make him the drama's most unsettling villain. Actually the legitimate heir to the throne, disfigured in a palace fire and raised as Prince Changxin's son.

Fan Changning 范长宁

Shi Sanniang 石三娘

🏠 Arc 1: The Fake Marriage (Ep 1–6)

EP 1–6

Snow, Survival & a Wedding

EP 1Bandits kill Fan parents. Changyu finds a wounded man in the snow — Xie Zheng, fallen Marquis of Wu'an. He lies: "I'm Yan Zheng, a refugee." She takes him in.
EP 2Greedy uncle & thugs try to seize the house deed. Neighbors suggest a matrilocal husband to protect the property. Yan Zheng overhears and agrees — without her even asking.
EP 3They set terms: she'll hire doctors, he'll leave when recovered. Court hears Marquis Wu'an may be dead. Two factions (Wei vs Li) search secretly for his body.
EP 4Uncle files petition. Yan Zheng helps Changyu with legal docs. Ex-fiancé Song Yan offers to make her concubine — she refuses. Yan Zheng kicks him out. Accidental kisses. WEDDING DAY — everyone stunned by the groom's beauty.
EP 5Fake consummation (candle shadows). Song family tries to reclaim betrothal money. Yan Zheng secretly bought back her pawned jade hairpin — the one she pawned for his medicine.
EP 6Daily life deepens. Neighbors, sister Changning, and Yan Zheng form a warm domestic unit. The pig shop opens for business.

Fan Changyu — the pig butcher who became a general 🐷⚔️

⚔️ Arc 2: Assassins & Secrets (Ep 7–12)

EP 7–12

Iron Suicide Warriors & Hidden Identities

EP 7Uncle found dead. Changyu arrested. Wei family's elite assassins attack — Yan Zheng & Changyu fight together. Gongsun Yin arrives with forged travel docs to free them.
EP 8Gongsun Yin discovers his best friend is now a butcher's matrilocal husband! 🤣 We learn: Xie Zheng was ambushed while investigating the Jinzhou massacre (17 yrs ago). Yu Qianqian offers Changyu a meat supply deal.
EP 9Fake consummation #2 (neighbors eavesdropping 😂). MORE assassins! Chancellor Wei sent them for a letter the Fan family has. Li Huai'an arrives, suspects Yan Zheng is the Marquis. General He sends a Tiger Seal to Wei Yan as leverage.
EP 10General He is Changyu's father's old comrade. Li Huai'an moves into Lin'an to "investigate." He and Yan Zheng have history — trained together under General He. Tension builds.
EP 11Yan Zheng warns Li Huai'an: "Don't fence-sit." Secret meeting with Black Robed Cavalry. Yu Qianqian is ambushed — reveals a hidden underground room where her son Bao'er lives. Romance seeded: Gongsun Yin ♥ Qi Shu.
EP 12Qianqian's mysterious past deepens. She has a son, pursued by wealthy rice merchant Qi Min. Changyu bonds with Qianqian.

The toxic but magnetic Qi Min & Yu Qianqian — obsession vs independence 💔

💔 Arc 3: The Truth Unravels · Farewell (Ep 13–19)

EP 13–19

Family Secrets, Massacre & Separation

EP 13Qianqian borrows Yan Zheng to pose as her husband to meet Qi Min. Yan Zheng recognizes the name — Qi Min hoarded 200K shi of grain. Qianqian advises Changyu to have a child with him. 💡
EP 14New Year. Kids (Bao'er & Changning) get lost. Qi Min's spy learns Bao'er's true birthdate — SHOCK: BAO'ER IS QI MIN'S CHILD! Gongsun Yin paints a family portrait. Yan Zheng plans to leave in 3 days.
EP 15SHOCK: Changyu's "grandfather" reveals her father was NOT his biological son. Real father paid to be adopted 17 yrs ago. Parents left a suicide note (burned by uncle). Yan Zheng decides it's time to leave.
EP 16Sui Yuanqing (Prince Changxin's son) leads a riot. Yan Zheng's Blood Robe Cavalry intercepts the grain. Changyu rescues Qianqian from arrest, hides Bao'er. Yan Zheng fights Sui Yuanqing at a cliff — shoots him.
EP 17TIME TO PART. Changyu gives him money, clothes, and a DIVORCE LETTER. He's furious. They argue, he kisses her forcefully, she slaps him. "Wait a year for me." He leaves. She finds his birthday gifts → breaks down. Bandits attack Lin'an.
EP 18BRUTAL MASSACRE. Sui Yuanqing (alive!) hired bandit chief Shi Sanniang to massacre Lin'an. Constable Wang, Chef Li, neighbors, Magistrate Cui — ALL KILLED. Changyu lured bandits away, cornered at cliff, jumps off. Xie Zheng arrives, finds her hanging on vines. 🥺
EP 19Xie Zheng performs scraping therapy to save her. Promises to marry her. But war calls — he must lead his army. Changyu starts searching for her sister Changning (taken by rebels).

🗡️ Arc 4: Battlefield · Reunion (Ep 20–25)

EP 20–25

The Northwest Pig Butcher Squad

EP 20Changyu becomes "Butcher Beauty of Jizhou" — freeing trafficked children. Arrested by mistake; Li Huai'an bails her out. Sui Yuanqing sends Xie Zheng the family portrait + threat.
EP 21Bao'er is traumatized by Sui Yuanhuai's cruelty. Changyu in a labor camp with elderly scholar Tao Yi (secretly Xie Zheng's teacher & former Grand Tutor!) and the Jin brothers.
EP 22Changyu's superhuman strength: carries 2 baskets of stone up a mountain! 🏔️ Tao Yi reveals the plan: blow the dam to flood enemies. Tao Yi sacrifices himself by swapping lots. Xie Zheng saves Changning. DAM EXPLODES. 💥
EP 23Northwest Pig Butcher Squad kills enemy spies. Xie Zheng doesn't realize it's Changyu's group. He sends a bird: "Changning is saved." Changyu volunteers to lead supply team.
EP 24Princess Qi Shu arrives as physician. Changyu reunites with Changning! Works in medical tent. REUNITED with Xie Zheng — but she still calls him "Yan Zheng."
EP 25Qi Shu warns Changyu about "handsome men who lie." Changyu and Xie Zheng share tender moments. He still hasn't revealed his identity.

🔥 Arc 5: Identity Revealed · The War (Ep 26–33)

EP 26–33

The Mask Comes Off

EP 26BIG REVEAL: Changyu drugs Xie Zheng's medicine, puts on his uniform, fights in his place. Kills General Shi Hu with his own mace! Xie Zheng rides in — soldiers cheer "Marquis Wu'an!" Changyu realizes: HER HUSBAND IS THE MARQUIS. He picks her up and rides off. 🐎
EP 27Forest confrontation. She's furious he lied. He explains: kept identity to protect her. She rejects his proposal — social status gap. Xie Zheng begs Tao Yi to adopt Changyu as god-daughter.
EP 28Tao Yi adopts her, names her "Shanjun" 山君. Changyu leaves camp to find Qianqian & Bao'er. THE BIG TRUTH: Bao'er is the TRUE Imperial Grandson. Crown Princess swapped him with Sui Yuanhuai during the palace fire. "Qi Min" is the rightful heir!
EP 29Qianqian drugs Sui Yuanhuai, escapes. Reunites with Changyu. General He gives Changyu custom armor and twin blades forged by Xie Zheng with his blood.
EP 30Changyu takes over Li Huai'an's troops (he's wounded). BATTLE OF LUCHENG — Changyu faces Prince Changxin, kills him. DYING REVELATION: "Wei Qilin is a traitor!" Gen. He dies. Wei Qilin = Changyu's father.
EP 31Memorial for Xie Zheng's father + funeral for Gen. He. Crowd blames Wei Qilin. Changyu defends him without revealing she's his daughter. Promoted to 5th rank Commandant.
EP 32Emperor orders marriage between Xie Zheng and Princess Qi Shu. Xie Zheng rejects it, cuts off a eunuch's ear. He wants Bao'er (true heir) to be future Emperor.
EP 33Xie Zheng investigates the 17-year tragedy. Changyu keeps distance, calls him "Your Lordship." Political factions sharpen.

🏛️ Arc 6: Capital · Rebellion · Finale (Ep 34–40)

EP 34–40

The Final Battle for Truth & Love

EP 34Back to the capital. Wei Yan vs Li family court battle. Changyu promoted to "Flower-Crowned General" (4th grade). Grand Tutor Li plans to crown Qi Min. Li Huaian put under house arrest for disagreeing.
EP 35Qianqian & Bao'er found and caught by Qi Min. Changyu gets etiquette lessons from ex-fiancé Song Yan. Xie Zheng: "Changyu is the jade." 💎
EP 36Xie Zheng's triumphant return. Changyu throws her hair tie instead of silk handkerchief. Wei Yan canes Xie Zheng 180 times (family law) for marrying a "traitor's daughter." Qianqian steals Qi Min's tiger tally (it's FAKE!).
EP 37Palace banquet. Aphrodisiac trap! Gongsun Yin performs CPR to save Qi Shu (modern technique taught by Qianqian the transmigrator!). Xie Zheng kills the corrupt Eunuch Li.
EP 38They finally consummate their marriage. 🔥 Qi Min's fake tiger tally revealed. Changyu strikes the DENGWEN DRUM — imperial appeal to reopen her father's case. People throw vegetables at her. Xie Zheng finds his mother's letter proving Wei Yan abandoned the army.
EP 39COURT CLIMAX: Wei Yan claims letter & seal are fake. Tao Yi confirms seal is real. QI MIN STORMS THE PALACE with soldiers, sits on the throne! Xie Zheng & Wei Yan fight together. Qi Min falls from wall — Qianqian falls too. Changyu catches her. ULTIMATE TRUTH: The LATE EMPEROR set the fire, replaced water with oil, lured Wei Yan back.
EP 40Wei Yan kills himself to quell the rebellion. The young Emperor (Bao'er) ascends. Xie Zheng becomes REGENT. Fan Changyu becomes GENERAL. Yu Qianqian becomes EMPRESS DOWAGER. They step away from power → return to LIN'AN TOWN for a quiet life together. "True victory is not in ruling, but in surviving without losing yourself." Happy ending. Final Easter egg: a "what if" alternate scene.

🔮 The 17-Year Jinzhou Mystery

How It All Connects

LATE EMPEROR feared Crown Prince Chengde's growing power. AT A PALACE BANQUET: Young Wei Yan said "forbidden words" about abdicating the Emperor. → 5 people heard it: Wei Yan, Crown Prince Chengde, Tao Yi, Xie Linshan (Xie Zheng's father), Wei Qilin (Changyu's father) THE LATE EMPEROR'S SCHEME: 1. Set fire to Qingyuan Palace 2. Replaced water with oil so it couldn't be extinguished 3. Consort Shu's letter to Wei Yan was a SETUP → to lure Wei Yan back to the capital 4. Crown Prince & Xie Linshan DIED in battle 5. Wei Qilin fled to Lin'an, changed name to Fan Erniu 6. Xie Zheng survived, fell from cliff → rescued by Changyu THE PALACE FIRE (16 years ago): Crown Princess swapped the real heir (Qi Min) with Prince Changxin's son (Sui Yuanhuai). Disfigured Qi Min's face with fire. → Qi Min grew up as "Sui Yuanhuai" → True heir grew up as "Qi Min" — rightful Imperial Grandson "Qi Min" (the rice merchant) = Sui Yuanhuai in disguise His son Bao'er = Qi Min's child = Imperial Great-Grandson THE ENDING: ✅ True heir (Bao'er) becomes Emperor ✅ Wei Yan suicides to end the cycle ✅ Xie Zheng chooses love over power ✅ Changyu avenges her father and chooses peace
